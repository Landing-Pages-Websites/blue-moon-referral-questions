Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy describes how Blue Moon Senior Counseling handles information submitted at https://info.bluemoonseniorcounseling.com, including https://info.bluemoonseniorcounseling.com/get-started. The main website at https://bluemoonseniorcounseling.com does not publish a separate privacy policy.

Information we collect

On the get-started form we collect the name, email address, and phone number you enter, plus any optional note you choose to add. We also collect technical information such as the page address, browser, and approximate location derived from an IP address. Do not include a diagnosis, Medicare number, insurance number, or other medical details in the optional note.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to respond to your request, schedule a conversation, and keep ordinary business records. A phone number is collected so the team can call you back. Entering a phone number is not consent to text. Visiting the website is not consent to text.

How we share information

We share information with service providers that host the website, deliver form submissions, and place calls or, when you have opted in, text messages. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect safety. We do not sell contact information.

SMS / Text Messaging

Text messages from Blue Moon Senior Counseling are optional. On https://info.bluemoonseniorcounseling.com/get-started, both forms include an SMS checkbox that is unchecked. The phone field is required so the team can call you back. Checking the box is not required to submit the form. Entering a phone number is not consent to text. Visiting the website is not consent to text.

If you check the box, you agree to receive customer-care text messages from Blue Moon Senior Counseling about your inquiry, including follow-up and scheduling. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Reply START to resubscribe after you opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
